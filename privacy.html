Eulogia Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026

Eulogia (ആശംസകൾ) is an Android app made by Shaji Antony. It helps you remember birthdays, wedding anniversaries and church remembrances of the people you care for. This page explains what the app does with your information.

1. Information you add

You may add names, dates of birth and marriage, spouse names, phone numbers, email addresses, city and country, group and position details, photos and greeting posters. You type this in yourself or import it from an Excel file you choose.

2. Where it is stored

All of this information is stored only on your phone, inside the app. It is not sent to the developer or to any server run by the developer. The app has no user accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

3. Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose to turn on backup, you sign in with your Google account. The app then asks Google for two things only:

  • Your email address, to show which account the backup uses.
  • Access to the app's own hidden folder in your Google Drive (Drive "appDataFolder"). The app cannot see or change any of your other Drive files.

The backup is a single file containing your Eulogia data and photos. It is stored in your own Google Drive and only you have access to it. The developer never receives it. You can stop backup at any time with Settings › Disconnect, and remove the app's Drive access from your Google Account settings. Google's handling of your account is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Eulogia's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Sharing

The app shares a greeting or poster only when you tap Share or Wish, and only to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp). Nothing is shared automatically.

5. Permissions

  • Notifications and alarms: to remind you on the day and the day before.
  • Run at start-up: to set reminders again after the phone restarts.
  • Internet: used only for Google sign-in and Drive backup.

6. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes all data from your phone. To delete your backup, open Google Drive on the web › Settings › Manage apps › Eulogia › Options › Delete hidden app data. To stop the app's access, go to your Google Account › Security › Third-party apps with account access › Eulogia › Remove access.

7. Children

Eulogia is not directed at children, and the developer does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
